How to read it

Electricity creates an advantage, not a verdict.

China’s western hubs have a clear electricity-price advantage over the US markets shown here. The advantage is meaningful, but it is not unique: the published Quebec and Saudi rates are lower.

This chart deliberately removes chip access, model efficiency and construction cost. It measures the geographic value of cheap power if every location can run the same equipment.

FLOPs per $ = 180 PFLOP/s × 3,600
              / (120 kW × 1.20 PUE × electricity $/kWh)

Model choice does not change raw FLOPs per dollar. It matters in a separate analysis of useful model output per dollar.

What is factual here

Every price and capacity observation links to its source. Planned capacity is excluded. Currency conversions use official exchange-rate sources.

What remains a counterfactual

The same GB200 rack, PUE and utilization are imposed everywhere. Actual chip access, hardware mix and useful model output are outside this curve.
