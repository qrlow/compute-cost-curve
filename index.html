Not one price

Reported western-hub prices are much lower than eastern grid-tariff proxies. “China electricity cost” is a distribution.

How to read it

Cheap hubs are real. Applying their price to all Chinese capacity is not.

Hohhot and Gui’an use reported or policy-backed data-center prices only for capacity that can be matched to those hubs. The remaining provincial blocks use published high-voltage grid tariffs plus a transparent demand-charge calculation.

The dashed lines are price benchmarks only. They do not claim that selected US markets and national Chinese capacity have comparable measurement boundaries.

Capacity denominator

10.43 million standard racks × 2.5 kW = 26.075 GW. Only the ten largest provincial estimates—18.203 GW—are priced in this edition.

Cost denominator

Tariff blocks are transparent counterfactual bills for a large, steady high-voltage load. They are not claimed hyperscaler contracts.

Remaining uncertainty

The other 21 provinces and a national US capacity denominator are omitted until the same source and price boundaries can be assembled.
